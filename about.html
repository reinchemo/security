About Maxis Security

Our Story

Built on Trust, Integrity & Professionalism

Maxis Security was founded in 2016 with a simple mission: to provide the Lower Mainland with reliable, professional, and responsive security services. What started as a small team of dedicated security professionals has grown into a trusted partner for businesses, construction sites, residential communities, and events across British Columbia.

We believe that security is about more than just watching over property—it's about building relationships, understanding risks, and providing peace of mind. Our team is committed to delivering customized solutions that meet the unique needs of each client.
